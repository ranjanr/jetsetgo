## 🛠️ Execution Playbook

### 1. Modifying Steps
Submit your raw notes, interview findings, or hypothesis drafts to any step by sending them in our chat conversation. Format your input indicating which step you are working on, e.g.:
> **Step 1 Submission:** We did 15 primary interviews with corporate travel coordinators...

### 2. State & Re-sync rules
- **Data Isolation**: Updates to parent steps do not automatically overwrite down-stream data.
- **Flagging Outdated Steps**: If you update **S1**, the down-stream steps (**S2**, **S3**, **S4**, and **S6**) will be marked with a `Resync Required` flag (⚠️) to warn of potential misalignment.
- **Explicit Re-Sync Trigger**: Send `/resync` in the chat to run alignment checks and reconcile the flags.

### 3. The 3-Agent Evaluation Route
Whenever you submit info or request a resync, three agent layers run to analyze your business:
1. **Critic Agent**: Detects confirmation biases, logical leaps, or unverified metrics (prepending `[!! CRITIC ALERT: Unverified Synthetic Metric]` for unsourced statistics).
2. **Structure/Research Agent**: Formats data into clean markdown tables, timelines, or diagrams based strictly on facts you supply.
3. **Synthesizer Agent**: Polishes output into crisp, actionable, execution-focused plans.
